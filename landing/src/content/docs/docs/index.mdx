ReplayVOD is a self-hosted app for recording and watching Twitch streams. Record live streams on demand, set up automatic recording, or save past broadcasts from Twitch. Watch your recordings in your browser, with files stored on your server or in S3-compatible storage.

Each viewer signs in with Twitch and has their own watch progress, watch later list, and favourite channels.
