title: Your account
description: Change your display preferences and manage your signed-in devices.
---

Open your account menu at the top right of the dashboard to change settings, review sessions, or sign out.

## Display preferences

Open **Settings**, change your preferences, and select **Save**.

| Setting | Choices |
| ------- | ------- |
| Language | English or French. |
| Time zone | A time zone such as `Europe/Brussels`. The default is UTC. |
| Date and time format | ISO (`2026-04-12 15:30`), EU (`12/04/2026 15:30`), or US (`04/12/2026 3:30 PM`). |

These settings apply to your account. To switch between light and dark themes, use the theme control in your account menu. The theme changes immediately.

## Where you're signed in

Open **Sessions** to see the browsers and devices signed in to your account. Each session shows when it was last used and when it expires. Your current session is marked **current**.

Select **Revoke** to sign a session out. Revoking the current session signs you out of the dashboard you're using.

## Sign out

Select **Sign out** in your account menu to end the session in this browser. Other sessions stay signed in. Your watch progress and saved lists remain available when you return.

An admin can change your role or remove your access. Removing your account from the whitelist also signs out your sessions. See [Users & invitations](/docs/access/).
