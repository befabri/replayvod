---
title: Watching recordings
description: Find recordings, resume watching, and save videos for later.
---

Open **Library → Videos** to browse recordings. Everyone signed in to the server can use the library.

## Find a recording

Use the tabs above the list to choose what to show:

| Tab | Recordings shown |
| --- | ---------------- |
| **All** | All recordings in the library. |
| **This week** | Recordings from the last seven days. |
| **Unwatched** | Recordings you haven't started watching. |
| **Watch later** | Recordings you've saved for later. |

Use **Sort** to order recordings by recording date, stream date, channel, duration, or size. **View** switches between posters and a table.

Open **Filters** to narrow the list:

| Filter | Choices |
| ------ | ------- |
| Status | Done, Running, Pending, Failed. |
| Quality | Recording resolution. |
| Language | Broadcast language. |
| Duration | Under 30m, 30m–2h, 2h–4h, or 4h+. |
| Type | **Live recordings** or [**Archives**](/docs/archive/). |

The search in the header searches videos, channels, and categories across the library. You can limit the results to any one of those groups.

## Pick up where you left off

Open a recording you've started and select **Resume at** to continue from your saved position, or **Start over** to play from the beginning. The resume prompt appears once you've watched a few seconds and disappears when you're near the end.

The **Continue watching** row on the **Dashboard** lists recordings you've started but haven't finished, with the most recently watched first.

Watch progress is saved to your account. Other people's viewing doesn't affect your progress or the **Unwatched** tab.

## Save something for later

Select **Add to watch later** on a recording to save it to your **Watch later** tab. Select **Remove from watch later** to remove it from the list. This list is private to your account.

## What the badges mean

| Badge | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| **Archive** | Downloaded from a past broadcast on Twitch. |
| **PARTIAL** | Some of the recording is missing. The captured footage is still playable. |
| **TRUNCATED** | Recording stopped before the broadcast ended. |
| **CANCELLED** | Someone cancelled the recording. |

## While you watch

If a recording has several parts, the player switches between them automatically. The [playback cache](/docs/playback-cache/) lets you seek across compatible parts as one video. The server owner can enable it.

The **Stream timeline** shows title and category changes captured during the broadcast. Select a marker on the seek bar to jump to that change. Use the layout control to place the timeline beside or below the player. See [Title tracking](/docs/schedules/#title-tracking) for how this history is recorded.

## Channels

Open **Library → Channels** to browse broadcasters. The tabs show **All**, **Live now**, **Downloaded**, or **Favorites**. A **Live** badge means the channel is currently streaming.

Select a channel's star to add it to **Favorites**. Your favourites are saved to your account. To record a channel automatically, create or request a [schedule](/docs/schedules/).

Open a channel to see its recordings. Admins and owners can also select **Download** to record a stream that's live now.

## Categories

Open **Library → Categories** to browse recordings by Twitch category. Each category shows its artwork and recording count. Sort by the most recent recording or the number of recordings, then open a category to see its videos.
